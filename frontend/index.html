HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Reduce your opponent to less than 3 pieces or block all of their legal moves.

PHASE 1: PLACING

Players take turns placing their 9 pieces one by one onto empty points on the board.

PHASE 2: MOVING

Once all 9 pieces are placed, players move one piece per turn along a line to an adjacent empty spot.

PHASE 3: FLYING

When a player is reduced to exactly 3 pieces, their pieces can "fly" to ANY vacant point on the board!

FORMING A MILL

Line up 3 pieces in a straight row (horizontal or vertical) to form a MILL. This allows you to remove one opponent piece from the board!
